4 · Recovery to passcode new message

If the passkey was deleted, or the server passcode was changed, the vault is cleared and the normal passcode sheet appears with one explanatory line. Nothing else changes.

What does not change

Customer view needs no unlock. Devices that haven't turned this on behave exactly as in 2.6.2, including the passcode sheet and the Lock button. Navy #141943 and white only; no new colours except the existing --warn tokens for the recovery note.

States with no screen of their own

Unsupported device (iOS ≤17, or a passkey manager without PRF): the card, the Settings section and the biometric button are never rendered. User cancels the Face ID prompt: stays on screen 2, no error banner. Offline unlock: identical to screen 2, then straight into the saved catalogue.
